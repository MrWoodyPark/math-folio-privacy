Math Folio Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated November 25, 2026

Math Folio provides offline math practice. WoodyyVerse does not collect or transmit your answers, handwriting, practice history or device identifiers. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics SDKs or tracking.

Your notebook

Your device stores your selected level, current page, answers, attempts, hints and scratchpad strokes so you can resume practice. Deleting the app removes its local notebook. Device backups may retain a copy according to your backup settings.

External services

Opening this policy visits a website hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes web requests under its own privacy policy. If you email us, your email provider delivers the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your inquiry.
